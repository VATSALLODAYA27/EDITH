# Nimbus Labs — Travel & Expense Policy

## Meals
While travelling for work, meals are reimbursed up to ₹1,500 per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client dinners may exceed the limit with prior manager approval.

## Flights and hotels
Book economy class for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 6 hours. Hotels are reimbursed up to ₹7,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,500 elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses
Submit all receipts in the Expenses portal within 30 days of the expense. Claims above ₹20,000 need manager approval before reimbursement. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.
