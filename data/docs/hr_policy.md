# Nimbus Labs — HR Policy

## Vacation
Every full-time employee gets 24 paid vacation days per year. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; the rest expire on 31 December. Vacation requests longer than 5 consecutive days need manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers get 4 weeks. Leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are in-office "collaboration days" for all teams. Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department head.
