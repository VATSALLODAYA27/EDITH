# Nimbus Labs — Offices

## Pune (headquarters)
Open 9am–7pm, Monday to Friday. Visitors must register at reception and wear a visitor badge. Parking is available in basement level B2; request a parking pass from the admin team.

## Bangalore
Open 10am–8pm, Monday to Friday. The Bangalore office hosts the Data team and the Design team. The guest Wi-Fi network is "Nimbus-Guest"; ask reception for the daily password.

## Office closures
Both offices are closed on national holidays. The holiday calendar for the year is published on the intranet every December.
